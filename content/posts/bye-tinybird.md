June 30 was my last day at Tinybird. After seven wonderful and intense years, I'm moving on.

I need to recharge, and to find that excitement again for building new products and/or for helping others chase their dreams.

There are a lot of people to thank.

To everyone who has worked or still works at Tinybird, for believing in our dream and trusting us, the founders. The hardest part, without a doubt, was letting great professionals and great people go, for one reason or another.

To our customers. Without you we couldn't have grown, or taken on bigger and bigger challenges.

To our investors: [Crane](https://crane.vc), [CRV](https://www.crv.com), [Singular](https://singular.vc) and [Balderton](https://www.balderton.com). To get anywhere you need fuel, and without it, it's hard.

And finally, to my travel companions, [Santana](https://x.com/javisantana), [Raúl](https://x.com/rochoa), [Sergio](https://x.com/saleiva) and [Sancha](https://x.com/jorgesancha). Our relationship has changed after this adventure, which could fill a book, for the good and for the bad, but I don't regret having lived it with you.

I'll miss the team, the laughs, the office, and the founders' trips where we argued about everything (and yes, you have to argue).

🌊🌊🌊🌊
