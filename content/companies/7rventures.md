## What 7R was

7R Ventures operated as a **venture builder** and **shadow management** partner for founders and executives: **hands on** help to turn early traction into **profitable, structured growth**, often from the background rather than as a loud agency pitch. Their positioning today is still along those lines. See the [7R site](https://www.7r.ventures) for how they frame the work.

## Role

I helped **design and build prototypes** so ventures could **validate and launch** with something real in users’ hands, not slideware. Once the **MVP was in place**, I supported **hiring the technical team** so the company could own delivery with a clear product and stack already on the rails.

## How I worked

- **Prototype to launch:** short cycles focused on shippable software, not endless discovery.
- **Hiring with context:** engineers interviewed against a codebase and product that already existed, so expectations and bar were concrete.
- **Founder bandwidth:** reduce friction between “we have an idea” and “we have a team shipping.”
